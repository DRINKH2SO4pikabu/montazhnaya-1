## Visual review checklist

- Inspect at 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% Windows display scaling.
- Check whether top-bar menus wrap without overlapping the toolbar.
- Check property labels and number inputs side by side; labels must not push controls off-screen.
- Check modals and menu popovers at the minimum supported window size.
- For Arabic/Hebrew, confirm reading order while the timeline ruler and clip geometry remain left-to-right.
- Check glyph coverage and font fallback for Georgian, Armenian, Devanagari, Thai, CJK and Uzbek Cyrillic.
- Confirm keyboard shortcuts, numbers, file paths and codec names remain readable and unmirrored.
