# Code of Conduct

We want Montazhnaya_1 to be a welcoming project for people with different backgrounds, skills and levels of experience.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Provide context and reproducible steps when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's media, logs or personal information without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately derail other participants.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, threatening, discriminatory, spam or privacy-violating content and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

For safety-sensitive issues, use the private reporting route in `SECURITY.md` rather than posting exploit details publicly.
